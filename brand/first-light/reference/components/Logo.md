The Stein Product logo system: two lockups and one icon, all built on First Light, the view of a planet's horizon at sunrise from orbit.

**Provide:** optional `variant` (`signature` | `wordmark` | `tile` | `porthole`, default `wordmark`), `tone` (`auto` | `night` | `day`; `auto` follows the page theme), `height` in px (defaults: signature 64, wordmark 32, icons 40), `href`.

- `signature`: the horizon arcs over the whole name with the sun rising at center. Use where the logo is the main event: the site hero or footer sign-off, link previews, slide covers, merch. Minimum height 48px.
- `wordmark`: the everyday logo. The horizon spans "ein" and the sun sits over the i. Use in the site nav (28 to 32px tall), email signatures, documents and invoices. Minimum height 20px.
- `tile` and `porthole`: the icon on its own (planet limb, atmosphere, half-risen sun with a lens flare). Tile for favicons and app icons, porthole for avatars and social profiles.
- Dark first. The logo is made of light and belongs on `void`, `surface-1` or an atmosphere ground. Put it on white only when unavoidable, and there prefer carrying it in its own dark panel or using the tile. There is no flat or one-color version.
- The lettering is outlined Anybody Condensed ExtraBold. Never retype it, recolor the horizon, move the sun, stretch the arc or add effects.
