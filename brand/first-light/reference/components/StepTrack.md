A numbered process on an orbital line, with a beam of light traveling between steps.

**Provide:** `steps` (array of `{title, detail?, meta?}`), optional `active` (index of the current step, default 0), `beam` (false turns off the traveling light).

- Three to five steps. Titles are two to four words; details one short sentence.
- `meta` holds the time or cost, set in the signal face ("30 min", "Within a week").
- Done steps glow `aurora`; the active step holds the `sol` sun. Stacks vertically under 640px.
