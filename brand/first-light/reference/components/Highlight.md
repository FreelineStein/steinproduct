Paints a few words of display type with the Horizon gradient, like first light hitting the line.

**Provide:** `children` (the words to light up, usually the last one to three words of a headline).

- One Highlight per headline and at most two per page. It marks the payoff word ("automated.").
- Use only on `display-xl`, `display-l` or `headline` text; never on body copy.
- Night renders the full gradient; Day falls back to solid `stein-teal` for contrast.
