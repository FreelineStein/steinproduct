Frosted, rim-lit panel for services, case studies and grouped content; the brand's card.

**Provide:** `children` (a string becomes body copy; nodes render as is), optional `eyebrow`, `title`, `footer` (array of short mono facts such as a price and a timeline), `featured` (rotating Horizon rim light), `interactive` (lift and glow on hover), `as` (element, default `div`).

- Place GlassPanels over an `sp-atmosphere` or `Aurora` ground so the blur has something to catch.
- One `featured` panel per group: the recommended option.
- Titles in `title` style, body in `body` at `ink-muted`, footer facts in `data` style.
- Keep panels in rows of two or three with `space-5` gutters. No colored left borders, no icons stacked above titles.
